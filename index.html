Group reference portfolio

Oilfield applications

The broader Zhongfu business reference includes the technical areas below. Project scope, equipment and operating responsibilities depend on the application.

CO₂ stimulation

CO₂ injection and alternating CO₂ and water injection are group reference approaches for production enhancement. Reservoir conditions guide equipment, injection and monitoring requirements.

Enhanced oil recovery

The reference portfolio covers chemical, gas-based and thermal recovery approaches. Technique selection depends on a reservoir-specific assessment.

Fracturing

Stimulation concepts for low-permeability formations include fracture-face treatment and acid-based approaches. Materials and operating limits depend on the project design.

Acidising and injectivity

Near-wellbore treatments address blockage and formation damage. Reference approaches include retarded acid, composite treatments and diversion.

Water shutoff and profile control

Treatments aim to manage unwanted water production and improve injection distribution. Selection depends on the well and formation compatibility.

Scale removal and prevention

Chemical and ultrasonic concepts address deposits in wells, pipework and surface equipment. Water chemistry, materials and site conditions guide treatment selection.
